Privacy Policy - Solidarity

Your privacy is important to us. This privacy policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information when using our app. The barcode scanning functionality is entirely offline and does not require any personal data.

2. How We Use Your Data

As we do not collect any personal information, there is no data to use or store on our servers. All barcode scans happen on the device itself.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not share any data with third-party services. Our app functions entirely offline, ensuring your privacy is protected.

4. Data Security

Since we do not collect or store any personal information, there is no data to secure. We ensure that all processing happens locally on your device, reducing any risk to your data.

5. Your Rights

As no personal data is collected, there are no actions needed from you to manage your data. You can opt-out by uninstalling the app from your device.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this privacy policy. Any changes will be reflected here. Please review this page periodically for any updates.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about our privacy practices, feel free to contact us at abdudevs@gmail.com.
